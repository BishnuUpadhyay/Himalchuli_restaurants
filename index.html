About Us

Welcome to Himalchuli Bar & Grill

Bringing the true essence of Himalayan hospitality to Virginia, Himalchuli Bar & Grill offers an authentic culinary journey through the vibrant flavors of Nepal, Tibet, and India. Named after the majestic peaks of the Himalayas, our kitchen blends time-honored family recipes with traditional mountain cooking to create an unforgettable dining experience.

From our meticulously hand-folded, aromatic steamed Momo dumplings to the deep, smoky profiles drifting from our traditional clay tandoor oven, every single dish is crafted with purpose. We utilize hand-ground spices, fresh local ingredients, and heritage techniques to bring a perfect balance of comfort, warmth, and flavor to your table.
